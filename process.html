Process

A process built for people who’ve been burned by agencies.

Fixed prices agreed before work starts. Demos every week. Proof before production. And a written list of the things we won’t do. Here’s the whole playbook.

Step 1 · Free · 30 minutes

Strategy call

You talk, we map. Bring the workflow that frustrates you, the product idea, or the vague sense that “we should be using AI.” An engineer — never a salesperson — asks the questions that matter and gives you an honest first read on the spot.

◆ Honest feasibility read◆ Rough cost & timeline

Step 2 · Fixed price · 1–2 weeks

Blueprint

We map the workflow end to end, design the agent or system, define where humans approve and where AI acts, and write the success metrics into a spec. The spec locks the scope; the scope locks the price. Signed before a line of code.

◆ Signed spec◆ Fixed price◆ Success metrics◆ Data & permission map

Step 3 · 3–6 weeks typical

Build & prove

Weekly demos you’re encouraged to interrupt. Then the part most vendors skip: shadow mode — your agent does the real job in parallel while your team grades its output daily. It graduates to live work when the scorecard clears the bar you set in the blueprint. Not before.

◆ Weekly demos◆ Shadow-mode trial◆ Graduation scorecard

Step 4 · Monthly · cancel with 30 days’ notice

Run & grow

AI systems drift — models update, data shifts, volumes grow. We monitor around the clock, re-test accuracy nightly, tune costs, and send a one-page monthly report in plain English. Prefer independence? We hand over everything with training and runbooks instead.

◆ 24/7 monitoring◆ Nightly accuracy tests◆ Monthly plain-English report◆ Full handover option

Each step bears the weight of the next — skip one and the whole climb gets risky. That’s why we never skip shadow mode.

The guarantees

In writing, or it doesn’t count.

Fixed means fixed

If we underestimate the work, the overrun is our cost. New scope is quoted and approved before we build it — never invoiced after.

30-day quality window

Anything that doesn’t match the signed spec within 30 days of delivery, we fix free. Genuine defects within 3 months — same.

You own everything

Code, prompts, integrations, documentation, dashboards. There is no proprietary lock-in layer. We’d rather be re-hired than un-fireable.

Bad news travels fastest

If the evidence says an approach won’t work, you hear it the day we do — with what we’d try instead. Silence is the only failure we recognise.

Humans own consequences

Actions that move money, delete data, or reach your customers pause for human approval. That’s architecture, not policy — the capability isn’t in the agent’s permissions.

Honest “no” included

Expect a “not yet” or “not this way” on a good share of ideas — telling you early is the cheapest trust we’ll ever build.

Step one is free and takes thirty minutes.
