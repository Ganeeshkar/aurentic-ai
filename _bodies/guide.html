Site guide

How this website was built.

The business direction, branding, design system, interactions, tools, and process behind the Aurentic AI site — documented so the reasoning is as inspectable as the result.

1 · The business direction

Aurentic AI is a new AI company with two pillars: AI agents & intelligent automation (agents, workflow automation, RAG systems, copilots) and AI-powered software development (custom AI apps, SaaS, MVPs, web & mobile, integrations), wrapped in strategy and ongoing operations. Target customers: startups & founders and small-to-mid-size businesses — buyers who move fast, care about ROI, and have been burned by agency hype.

Positioning: “AI agents that actually get work done.” The market research (Agentic India, Vstorm, Reckonsys, A-Team, Innovatrix, HatchWorks, Team400, and Datamatics’ agentic-AI use-case library) showed most competitors selling capability lists; the gap is a company that sells outcomes with trust engineering built in — human approval gates, action logs, shadow-mode trials, nightly accuracy testing. Aurentic’s name encodes it: authentic + agentic.

The use-case architecture follows the pattern requested from Datamatics’ agentic AI pages: use cases organized by industry (Banking & Finance, Insurance, Healthcare, Manufacturing, Logistics, Retail & E-commerce) and by function (Finance & Accounting, Customer Support, Sales & Marketing, HR, IT & Operations) — implemented here as a live interactive explorer with 22 filterable use cases rather than static pages.

2 · Naming

Four candidate names were generated and checked against the .com registry in real time: clockworkai.com (taken), veyraai.com (taken), axionlabs.com (taken), aurenticai.com (available) — so Aurentic AI won on both meaning and domain availability. The wordmark sets a bone “A” monogram against the night, grounded by a single vermillion point — the lamp left on.

3 · Design system — “The Night Office”

The design rejects the default AI-startup aesthetic (teal/purple gradients, glassy panels, particle meshes) for something cinematic: a warm black #0D0C0B night-office world where the work keeps running after everyone goes home. Bone #EFEAE2 carries the type; one loud vermillion #FF4A1C is reserved for signal moments, and amber #E8A34D — the colour of lamplight — marks annotations and the human-approval gate, so warm colour literally marks human presence. Components sit like lit objects in a dark room: hairline borders, soft glows instead of hard shadows, film-grain texture over everything, and generous negative space. Typography: Instrument Serif (poster-scale editorial display with italic accent words) + Archivo (body and UI) + Space Mono (annotations, nav, data). The direction was drawn from real award-site research — full-bleed atmospheric heroes with bottom-anchored serif type, tiny technical annotations as jewellery, one hot colour temperature — studied live from studios like Locomotive and Basement and from Pinterest boards of award-winning typographic design.

4 · The interactive layer

Chosen with full creative freedom, the signature interactions:

① Cinematic hero — a full-bleed, custom-generated night-office photograph graded into the palette, with a slow parallax on scroll, word-by-word serif reveal, corner annotations, and a scroll cue: the after-hours mood set in one frame. ② Live agent demo — three switchable scenarios (support, finance, sales) that type out a real agent run on a terminal-style job card, including the amber human-approval gate, so visitors watch the product promise happen in eight seconds. ③ Use-case explorer — industry × function filtering across 22 cases, instant, no page loads. ④ ROI calculator — three sliders → animated hours / money / FTE outputs, with deliberately conservative math (65% automatable share) stated openly. Everything degrades gracefully under prefers-reduced-motion and works by keyboard.

5 · Page architecture & conversion strategy

Eleven routes: Home (immersive) · Services (two pillars + engagement models + FAQ) · Use Cases (the explorer) · Process (timeline + written guarantees) · About · Insights (two full essays + pipeline) · Contact · Privacy · Terms · 404 · this guide. The conversion spine is a single, low-friction ask repeated everywhere: the free 30-minute strategy call — appropriate for startup/SMB buyers who won’t book a “discovery workshop” but will take a free expert call. The ROI calculator warms the visitor with their own numbers immediately before the ask.

6 · Content honesty

Because the company is pre-launch, the site contains no fabricated client logos, testimonials, or case-study results. Credibility is built instead through: specific method (shadow mode, autonomy dial, permission maps), written guarantees, transparent calculator math, and demo content explicitly labelled as simulation. Real results replace and strengthen these as they arrive.

7 · Tools, assets & technology

Research: seven reference-company sites analyzed for positioning gaps; the Datamatics agentic-AI use-case library mapped for industry/function structure; 2026 design-trend research plus live visual research — Pinterest boards of award-winning typographic design and screenshots of award-level studio sites (Locomotive, Basement, Linear) rendered in a headless browser — informing the cinematic night-office aesthetic. Imagery: all five photographs are original GPT Image 2 generations (via the kie.ai API) in a consistent cinematic night grade — warm black shadows, amber lamplight, 35mm film grain: the desk-lamp hero, hands sorting documents, an aerial night city (use cases), an ascending amber-lit staircase (process), and a lighthouse over a night sea (the About metaphor: honest signal in the dark). One colour temperature, one world. Optimized progressive JPEGs, 43–168 KB. Stack: hand-written static HTML/CSS/JS — zero frameworks, ~420 lines of vanilla JS powering all four interactive systems; semantic HTML, skip links, ARIA labels, keyboard operability; per-page SEO/OG tags, sitemap, robots.txt; a small Python assembler keeping every route on one shared template; static deploy on surge.sh (production would point aurenticai.com at the same build).

8 · Iteration passes

Pass 1 — structure & copy: full information architecture, all page content, use-case data model. Pass 2 — design & motion polish: headless-browser screenshot reviews at desktop and mobile widths; hero grading, parallax and type pacing, contrast scrims over photography, calculator slider styling, grain and glow subtlety. Pass 3 — independent review: two separate AI review agents audited the site — an art-director agent judging the screenshots against award-site standards, and a copy-strategist agent auditing every page copy for consistency and over-claims — and their prioritized findings (17 in total) were applied before the final QA: link audit, console sweep, reduced-motion audit, three-breakpoint reflow, image budget, and live-deploy verification of every URL.
