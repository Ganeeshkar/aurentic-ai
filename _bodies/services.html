04 / Common questions

Asked before you ask.

How much does this cost?

The strategy call is free. The diagnostic is a fixed four-figure engagement. First agents and automations typically cost the equivalent of one quarter of a senior hire — and unlike a hire, they work nights, weekends, and holidays. MVPs and SaaS builds are quoted from the signed spec. Every number is fixed in writing before work begins.

Which AI models and tech do you use?

We're model-agnostic on purpose: during the blueprint stage we benchmark leading models against your actual tasks and pick on evidence — accuracy, speed, cost, and data-residency needs. Engineering is in Python and TypeScript, deployed to your cloud where possible, integrated with the tools you already run.

Is our data safe?

Data boundaries are agreed in writing before any code: where data lives, which agents can read what, retention, and deletion. Agents get least-privilege credentials. Your data is never used to train public models. Your security team gets the permission map, not a trust-us paragraph.

What if the AI makes a mistake?

We design for it rather than pretend it won't happen: consequential actions (money, deletions, customer-facing sends) pause for one-click human approval; every action is logged, and anything that can be rolled back, is; and agents are re-tested nightly against benchmark tasks so drift is caught by us, not by your customers.

Will this replace our team?

Our agents take over queues, not jobs — the repetitive 65% that nobody was hired to love. Teams end up doing more of the judgment work they were actually hired for. We're straightforward about this in scoping: if the math only works as a headcount story, we'll say so plainly and let you make the human decisions.

We already have developers. Can you work with them?

Gladly — it's our favourite setup. Your team knows the domain; we bring the AI-systems experience. Every engagement ends with documentation, runbooks, and handover training, so the capability stays with you either way.
