Privacy policy.

Short and readable — because this site was built to collect almost nothing.

Effective: July 2026Scope: this website

The short version

This is a static informational website. It has no user accounts, no analytics trackers, no advertising pixels, no cookies set by us, and no backend database. We cannot misuse your data because we do not collect it.

What touches your browser

Hosting logs: our static-site host may keep standard server logs (IP address, user agent, pages requested) for security and capacity purposes, under its own retention policy. Fonts: typefaces load from Google Fonts, so your browser requests font files from Google’s servers under Google’s privacy policy; the production build can self-host fonts to remove even this. Everything else — animations, the agent demo, the use-case explorer, the ROI calculator — runs entirely in your browser and sends data nowhere.

The contact form

Submitting the form stores nothing on any server — it opens your own email app with a pre-filled draft addressed to us. If you send that email, we use it only to reply, and you join no mailing lists without explicitly asking to.

Your rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, India’s DPDP Act, and similar laws, you may request access to or deletion of personal data an organization holds about you. This site holds none. If you’ve emailed us and want the thread deleted, tell us and it’s gone within 30 days.

Changes

Material updates will be posted here with a new effective date. Before commercial launch, this policy will be reviewed by counsel and updated to cover the production form handling and any analytics we choose to add — with consent, if we do.
